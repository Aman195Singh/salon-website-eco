# Product Vision — Salon Business Automation

**Document status:** Authoritative Product Vision  
**Current product:** Salon Website + Owner Management Web App + Operations  
**Current release target:** V2 — Phase 1: Appointment Booking Management MVP  
**Last updated:** 2026-10-25

## 1. Long-Term Vision

Build a practical, affordable, and maintainable business-automation ecosystem for India's micro, small, and medium-sized businesses (MSMEs). Solutions should reduce repetitive work, improve customer experience, and help businesses operate efficiently.

## 2. Starting Point

The salon and beauty industry is the first pilot. Start with one salon and learn from the real implementation before expanding.

## 3. Product Evolution

- **V1 / V1.5 — Salon website + owner management app (Released):** Public pages, controlled owner authentication, structured content editing with live preview and draft/publish, enquiry management, basic analytics, FAQ chatbot, and developer support. V1.5 added responsive mobile polish, email composer, CMS safety, RLS hardening, and legal pages.
- **V2 — Business Operations and Automation (Active):** Operational salon workflows starting with Appointment Booking Management MVP (`PRD-V2.md`), followed by payment integration, WhatsApp messaging, and customer CRM.
- **Later versions:** Multi-salon tenancy, reusable solutions across multiple salons and MSME industries.

These are progressive milestones with strict release boundaries. Multi-salon SaaS and subscription billing are future directions. Each release must have its own approved scope and acceptance criteria.

## 4. Product Principles

1. Start with one clear, useful pilot.
2. Keep each release's scope explicit and verifiable.
3. Avoid implementing speculative future features.
4. Prefer understandable, maintainable, reusable code where genuinely useful.
5. Prioritize security, privacy, accessibility, responsive design, and reliability.
6. Keep costs low and identify paid services and free-tier limits before depending on them.
7. Preserve working functionality when making changes.
8. Validate features before marking them complete.
9. Do not grant owner access through unrestricted public signup.
10. Keep owner-editable content separate from the fixed website structure.

## 5. Long-Term Success

The product should become useful to a real salon, be maintainable by a small team or solo developer, and provide evidence to guide future releases.
