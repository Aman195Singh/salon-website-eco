# 🎨 Salon Website V1 — Visual Assets & AI Prompts Guide

This document lists every picture, banner, gallery item, and visual asset needed to make the **Aura Luxe Salon** website look like an ultra-luxury, high-end destination.

---

## 🌟 Visual Theme & Aesthetics Summary
* **Color Palette:** Warm Charcoal (`#1a140e`), Champagne Gold (`#d4af37`), Warm Amber (`#b8860b`), Soft Cream (`#fdfbf8`).
* **Photography Style:** Editorial beauty photography, warm soft natural lighting, shallow depth of field (cinematic bokeh), clean architectural lines, minimalist luxury interior with marble and gold brass accents.
* **Target Format:** High quality `.jpg` or `.webp` compressed under 400 KB for ultra-fast loading.

## 🏷️ Logo & Brand Identity

* **Recommended Logo:**
  - Minimalist geometric luxury monogram (`✦ Aura Luxe`).
  - Font: Clean serif or luxury sans-serif (`Cinzel` or `Playfair Display` or `Outfit`).
  - Colors: Metallic Champagne Gold `#d4af37` on dark or cream background.
* **Favicon:**
  - Minimalist sparkling four-pointed star `✦` in gold on dark circle background (already configured at `src/app/favicon.ico`).
